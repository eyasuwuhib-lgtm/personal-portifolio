About Me

I am Mekuriaw Wuhib Shumye, a dedicated Senior Nurse, Lecturer, and researcher at Wollo University, Department of Adult Health Nursing. With over 5 years of remarkable track record, I specialize in qualitative and quantitative data collection, supervision, analysis, dissemination, and publication.

My background includes substantial research and publication work, strengthening my expertise in evidence‑based practice and health systems research. I have led and contributed to numerous projects focusing on non‑communicable diseases, antimicrobial resistance surveillance, maternal health, and data quality improvement.

As a research publication and output officer, I am committed to translating research findings into actionable health policies and training the next generation of health professionals in advanced data analysis and research methodologies.
